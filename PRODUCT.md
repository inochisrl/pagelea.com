# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pagelea serves people who need to correct, sign, organize, or sanitize a PDF
without sending the document to a third party. They may be working at a desk on
a large monitor or making a time-sensitive correction from a phone or tablet.
The primary job is to open a document, make a precise change with confidence,
and export a verifiable result with as little interface friction as possible.

## Product Purpose

Pagelea is a free, open-source, local-first PDF workbench. Its editor makes
supported existing PDF text directly editable while keeping document bytes in
the browser. Success means the document is the obvious centre of the
experience, the next action is clear, every essential workflow works with
mouse, keyboard, touch, or stylus, and users can export without creating an
account or encountering an artificial task quota.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, capable. Pagelea should feel like a well-made professional
instrument: approachable on first use, quiet while someone is working, and
explicit whenever a PDF limitation could affect the result.

## Anti-references

- A document editor embedded inside a marketing-page card or constrained
  canvas.
- Desktop panels stacked into a long scrolling page on mobile.
- Hover-dependent controls, tiny touch targets, or hidden core functionality
  on touch devices.
- Decorative product chrome that competes with the PDF.
- Blind imitation of Sejda or another vendor's branding, visual assets, copy,
  or proprietary interaction details.
- Generic SaaS dashboards, gratuitous glass effects, and animation that delays
  document work.

## Design Principles

1. **The document owns the screen.** Once a workspace opens, the PDF receives
   the largest possible continuous viewport; marketing content leaves the task.
2. **Progressive disclosure, never feature removal.** Desktop may show pages,
   canvas, and properties together. Smaller screens expose the same functions
   through drawers and bottom sheets.
3. **Local-first confidence stays visible but quiet.** Privacy status should be
   easy to verify without becoming a repeated promotional banner.
4. **Precision is reversible.** Selection, undo, redo, page state, zoom, and
   export status must remain legible and predictable.
5. **Input parity is a product requirement.** Mouse, keyboard, touch, and
   stylus users get appropriately sized controls and equivalent paths through
   every essential action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the editor surface. Maintain a logical landmark and
focus order, visible focus states, keyboard editing and page-management
controls, labelled icon buttons, screen-reader status announcements, and
reduced-motion support. Interactive targets are at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels
for coarse pointers. Text and state indicators do not rely on colour alone.
The layout must remain usable at 320 CSS pixels wide, in mobile landscape, at
200% browser zoom, and when an on-screen keyboard reduces the visual viewport.
