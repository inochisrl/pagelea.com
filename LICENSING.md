## Contributions and relicensing

External contributions require acceptance of the
[Pagelea Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). Contributors retain copyright
in their work while granting Inochi SRL the rights needed to distribute the
combined project under the AGPL and, where appropriate, separate commercial
terms.

This requirement is deliberate: a Developer Certificate of Origin alone does
not grant the relicensing permissions needed for Pagelea's dual-licensing
model.

Until the CLA acceptance workflow has been reviewed and activated, maintainers
may discuss and review external patches but must not merge them.
